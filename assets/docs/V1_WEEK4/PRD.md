# What should be included in your presentation/pitch:

1. Project topic ( Give a brief overview and why you picked this topic)
2. Data Set(s)* information:
    - Describe your data set (how many classes/features/examples? What type of features?)
    - Why did you choose this data set ? Provide some  visualization, statistical summary  or any  pre-processing steps you have conducted.
    - What is the source of this data? Provide a link and explain how it has been previously used in the ML community.
    - Provide links to literature/references to support your study when possible.
3. Initial thoughts on your work plan and how you will meet the project requirements [R1-R5]
    - You may use a Gantt chart (not compulsory).
    - You can also prioritise tasks using MoSCoW analysis, while having a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) comprising the ‘Must’ backbone tasks in mind).
